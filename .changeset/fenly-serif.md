Fenly Serif is Seed's serif: display titles and reading text use it, with Domine and Noto Serif as fallbacks. It's Seed's own variable font (weight 200 to 900, roman and italic), built from Literata under the SIL Open Font License; `scripts/fenly-serif` rebuilds it. Type sizes don't change.
